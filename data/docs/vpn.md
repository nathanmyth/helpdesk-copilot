# VPN Access

_Fictional sample document for Northwind Labs. Last updated: 2026-09-15._

## Connecting to the VPN
Northwind Labs uses GlobalConnect VPN. Open the GlobalConnect app, enter the portal address vpn.northwind.example, and sign in with your company email and password. Approve the MFA push notification on your phone to finish connecting.

## Resetting your VPN password
Your VPN password is the same as your company single sign-on (SSO) password. To reset it, go to the self-service password portal at password.northwind.example, choose "Forgot password", and verify with your MFA device. The new password works on the VPN within 5 minutes.

## VPN keeps disconnecting
Frequent disconnects are usually caused by unstable Wi-Fi or an outdated client. Update GlobalConnect to the latest version from the Software Center, switch to a wired or stronger network, and restart the app. If disconnects continue for more than a day, file a ticket and include the time of each disconnect.

## VPN on personal devices
Personal devices may not connect to the VPN. Only company-managed laptops are allowed, per the Acceptable Use Policy.
