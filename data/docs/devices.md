# Laptops and Devices

_Fictional sample document for Northwind Labs. Last updated: 2026-07-20._

## New laptop setup
New laptops arrive pre-configured. Power on, connect to Wi-Fi, and sign in with your company email. Setup installs required apps automatically and takes about 45 minutes; keep the laptop plugged in.

## Laptop replacement
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. If your laptop is damaged or failing earlier, file a Hardware Issue ticket in the IT Service Portal. Back up your work to OneDrive before any replacement; local files are not migrated.

## Printing
Office printers are added automatically on company laptops. Send jobs to "Northwind-Secure-Print", then tap your badge on any printer to release them. Unreleased jobs are deleted after 24 hours.

## Wi-Fi in the office
Connect company laptops to the "Northwind-Corp" network; it signs in automatically with your device certificate. Guests and personal devices use "Northwind-Guest", which requires a daily access code from reception.
