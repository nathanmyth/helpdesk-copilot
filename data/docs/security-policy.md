# Security Policy Highlights

_Fictional sample document for Northwind Labs. Last updated: 2026-09-01._

## Reporting phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, reset your password immediately and call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357.

## Data storage
Company data must be stored in OneDrive, SharePoint, or approved systems. Do not store company data on personal cloud storage such as personal Dropbox or Google Drive accounts, or on USB drives.

## USB drives
USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. To share large files, use a OneDrive sharing link with an expiration date.

## Working from public places
On public Wi-Fi, always connect to the VPN before accessing company systems. Lock your screen (Windows + L or Control + Command + Q) whenever you step away.
