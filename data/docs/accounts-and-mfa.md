# Accounts and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

_Fictional sample document for Northwind Labs. Last updated: 2026-08-30._

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are changed every 180 days. You will get an email reminder 14 days before your password expires. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## Account locked out
Your account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or you can unlock it immediately at password.northwind.example using your MFA device.

## Setting up MFA on a new phone
Install the Authenticator app on the new phone. On a company laptop, go to myaccount.northwind.example, open Security Info, choose "Add sign-in method", and scan the QR code with the new phone. Remove the old phone from Security Info once the new one works.

## Lost or stolen phone
If you lose the phone with your MFA app, call the IT Service Desk immediately at extension 4357 so your MFA methods can be reset and your sessions revoked. Do not wait until the next business day.
